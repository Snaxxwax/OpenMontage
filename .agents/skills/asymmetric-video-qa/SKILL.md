# Asymmetric Video QA (Wrapper)

## Do this (in order)

1. Apply brand rules from `.Codex/AGENTS.md`.
2. Follow OpenMontage reviewer protocol: `skills/meta/reviewer.md`.
3. Apply the Asymmetric QA gates below.

## Asymmetric QA gates

- Leverage clarity: chokepoint + beneficiary + lever are explicit
- Narrative modes: uses at least two of Cartographer / Operator / Skeptic / Realist
- Retention pacing: meaningful change every 2–4s; no dead-air sequences
- Brand consistency: controlled, unsentimental, analytical; no hype
- Trust risk: no weak recommendations; disclosure included if affiliates exist
- Monetization fit: matches allowed categories; no junk offers
- “Private intelligence briefing” feel: structure/evidence/packaging carries authority
