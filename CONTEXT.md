## Title Engine Patterns

Three naming strategies locked as permanent Asymmetric title tools:

**Viral Vector**
Apply "— Exposed" as a terminal modifier when the actor is named, the mechanism is novel, and the frame is a reveal.
Pattern: `[The Actor] [Verb]ing [the System] — Exposed`
Example in use: `The Private Company Running Your Internet — Exposed`

**Trendjacking (Presence of Mind)**
Tie a durable leverage mechanism to a live cultural moment (election cycle, regulatory hearing, outage, crisis). The mechanism must be real and timeless — the cultural moment is the entry point, not the thesis.
Rule: The moment must be active or imminent. Past moments = recap, not reveal.
Example: `How Asymmetric AI Could Flip the 2026 Election`

**Absurdism**
A premise that sounds impossible but describes the real mechanism. The absurdity IS the point — not invented weirdness for clickbait. Must be literally true.
Test: If the premise sounds impossible but is factually accurate, it passes.
Example: `Hacking a Smart Toaster to Bypass a Corporate Firewall`

## Production Terms

**Chokepoint** — the single point in a system where one actor holds disproportionate control. The primary leverage type Asymmetric targets.

**Proof event** — a named, already-happened instance that demonstrates the chokepoint operating in the real world. Required in every episode. Cannot be hypothetical.

**Viral Vector** — a title modifier ("Exposed", future variants TBD) that signals a reveal structure. Actor named + mechanism described + modifier applied.

**Pacing DNA** — the measurable rhythm targets (WPM, visual events per window, first proof timing) extracted from reference video analysis before a script is written.

**Packaging test** — F2 gate where title, thumbnail, and viewer promise are locked before research begins. Operator must approve before F3.

**Cold open** — the opening mechanism sequence, ideally drawn from a concrete proof event already in the public record. Must establish conflict-in-progress before naming the episode's primary subject.
