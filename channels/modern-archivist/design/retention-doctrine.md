# Modern Archivist Retention Doctrine

## Channel target

Core References:
- Case File Documentary Styles: Coffeezilla, MagnatesMedia
- Visual Dynamics: case_file visual mode, critical_error state
- Visual Rhythm: visual change every 3-6 seconds
The Modern Archivist / Failure Ledger is a forensic business-crime documentary channel led by a sardonic digital archivist. The target output is not a research deck with narration; it is cinematic case-building that turns corporate and product failures into thesis-driven autopsies. The failure thesis leads; evidence defends it.

## Render and Post-Production Contract

### Render is NOT the Finish Line
Rendering an episode is merely the first milestone in the channel's quality and impact pipeline. The full production process includes:

1. Render: Initial video composition
2. Packaging: Metadata, thumbnails, chapter markers
3. Post-Publish Review: 
   - Community engagement analysis
   - Fact-checking verification
   - Legal compliance review
   - Performance metrics assessment

No episode is considered complete until it has passed the full packaging and post-publish review stages. The render is a draft, not a final product.

## Influence blend
- Coffeezilla: confrontation, evidence, contradictions, named stakes, and the moment where the record catches up to the claim.
- MagnatesMedia: cinematic pacing, escalating stakes, act turns, compression montages, and business-failure momentum.
- Modern Archivist: teal archive, crimson critical error, dry voice, flat case-file UI, deterministic Remotion animation, and a character who punctuates rather than hosts every second.
