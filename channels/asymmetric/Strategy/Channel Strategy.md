# Channel Strategy — Asymmetric (Current)

Operational strategy for building consistent Asymmetric videos using OpenMontage.

## Identity

- Channel: Asymmetric
- Tagline: **Where the leverage is.**
- Niche: Hidden systems and control surfaces in infrastructure, money, and technology

## Positioning Angles

1. Infrastructure Insider: how the physical + digital substrate works
2. Power Architect: who controls chokepoints and why
3. Systemic Historian: how old decisions created current dependencies

## Content Formats

- Flagship breakdowns: 15–30 min (subscriber conversion)
- Weekly evergreen: 8–12 min (library building)
- Intelligence briefings: 45–90s (discovery funnel)

## Retention Rules (Asymmetric)

- Hook must establish the chokepoint/control surface immediately
- No sequence holds an unchanged state longer than ~4 seconds
- Pattern interrupt every 8–15 seconds (reframe, reveal, swap label, new highlight)

## Production Stack (Local-First)

- OpenMontage repo: `/home/pop/OpenMontage-fresh`
- Playbook: `styles/asymmetric.yaml`
- Composition: Remotion or HyperFrames (pick at proposal time; both are available)
- Research: `Strategy/Perplexica Research.md` (local)
- Voice: start with local or configured TTS; upgrade voice only after cadence is proven
- Visuals: prefer flat vector assets + diagrams; only use video-gen when motion is essential

## Quality Gates

- Hook gate: first 30 seconds are high-signal and visually arresting
- Fact gate: no hallucinated technical details, dates, or names
- Style gate: semantic color rules upheld; no UI soup; mobile readability
